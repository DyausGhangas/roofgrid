Privacy, in plain language.

This policy explains what information RoofGrid may process when you use the website, planner, AI features, or contact options.

Information you choose to provide

RoofGrid processes the addresses, map locations, roof geometry, project assumptions, and solar-planning inputs you enter so it can produce layouts and estimates. If you use the AI assistant, your message and relevant project context are sent to the configured AI service. If you contact us, we receive the details you include in your message or contact form.

Location and map services

If you grant browser location permission, your device provides coordinates to the planner. Address searches and roof detection may send a query, coordinates, or a map bounding box to mapping and building-data services such as OpenStreetMap, Nominatim, Overpass, and Esri. NASA POWER receives coordinates used to retrieve climate information.

Service providers

RoofGrid may rely on hosting and analytics providers, mapping services, NASA POWER, the configured AI provider for optional chat, and FormSubmit to forward contact-form messages by email. These providers process information under their own privacy terms and may operate in different countries.

Storage and retention

Most planning interactions happen in your browser or through short-lived API requests. Hosting, analytics, AI, and contact providers may retain operational logs or submitted information according to their own policies. Exported project and PDF files are saved to the location you choose on your device.

Your choices

  • You can decline browser location access and search or move the map manually.
  • You can use the planner without sending a message to the AI assistant.
  • You can avoid the contact form and email us directly.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of contact information we control, subject to applicable law.

Children

RoofGrid is a technical planning tool and is not directed to children. Do not submit personal information through the contact form if you are not permitted to do so in your jurisdiction.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as RoofGrid’s features or service providers change. The effective date below identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the RoofGrid contact form.
