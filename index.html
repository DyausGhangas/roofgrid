How it works

From rooftop to solar plan in three steps.

Find your roof, design a practical panel layout, and estimate its energy, financial, and environmental impact.

01 / LOCATE

Find your rooftop

Search by address and inspect the satellite view. Select a detected building footprint or draw the roof boundary yourself.

02 / DESIGN

Plan the usable space

Mark obstacles, rooftop access, and maintenance paths. RoofGrid then arranges panels within the remaining usable area.

03 / ESTIMATE

Understand the impact

Estimate energy generation, installation cost, savings, payback period, and avoided carbon emissions using assumptions you can review and edit.
